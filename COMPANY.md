# Buda Marketplace

The official [Buda.im](https://buda.im) Marketplace — the source of truth for official Skills, Agents, and Teams available in Buda.

## What is Buda?

Buda is a multi-agent AI platform that lets you run a team of AI agents as your workforce. Each agent has its own persistent cloud workspace (Drive), and can operate a browser, terminal, and Git — all visible in one UI.
